Account & data deletion

Privacy Vault PRO does not require creating a login account. Core features work offline on your device.

If you configured an optional backend URL/API key, anonymized event logs may be sent to that server. You can delete all associated data as follows.

In-app deletion (recommended)

  1. Open Privacy Vault PRO.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete all my data and confirm.

This removes privacy logs, avatars, cache/temp files, API key, backend URL, and the app device identifier.

Web / email request

Email zub165@yahoo.com with subject Delete My Data. Include Android or iOS and approximate dates of use. We process requests within 30 days.

Dedicated deletion page: delete.html

Uninstall

Uninstalling the app removes sandbox data on the device.
