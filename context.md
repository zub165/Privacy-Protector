# Privacy Protector — Project context (website)

This GitHub repository hosts the **support/landing pages** for the Privacy Protector mobile app:

- `index.html`: homepage + features
- `Support.html`: support + FAQs
- `Privacy.htm`: privacy policy

## What the mobile app does (high level)

Privacy Protector is a **privacy toolkit** that focuses on **real actions that are allowed by iOS/Android**:

- **Erase in-app data**: delete Privacy Protector’s own documents/cache/temp and local preferences.
- **Emergency clear**: fast “wipe” of in-app logs and caches.
- **Duplicate cleanup**:
  - **Contacts**: scan + delete duplicates (with permission and confirmation).
  - **Photos**: exact duplicates + “similar photo” grouping (perceptual hash), with confirmation before deletion.
- **System handoff**: opens the correct system settings screens for actions that require OS control.

## What the app cannot do

- It cannot silently delete other apps’ private data.
- It cannot factory-reset the phone.
- It cannot lock third-party apps with a password unless the OS/manufacturer provides a built-in feature.
