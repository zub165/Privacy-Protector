Privacy tools that are honest about what they can do.

Privacy Protector is a privacy-focused mobile toolkit. It can permanently remove data created inside the app, find duplicates (contacts/photos), and guide you to the correct system screens for actions that require operating system control.

Get support Read privacy policy

What it can do (real)

On-device No cloud required
  • Erase in-app files: deletes app documents, cache/temp files, and local preferences.
  • Emergency clear: fast, aggressive reset of in-app data and caches.
  • Duplicate contacts: scans and deletes duplicates (keeps one, deletes the rest).
  • Duplicate photos: exact duplicates + “AI-like” similar-photo grouping (review before delete).
  • Network safety check: detects connection type and offers recommendations.

What it cannot do (by platform design)

Transparent Store-safe
  • It cannot silently wipe other apps’ private data. On Android it opens the app’s system info page; on iOS it shows steps.
  • It cannot factory reset your device. Only system Settings can do that.
  • It cannot “lock” other apps with a password unless the OS/manufacturer provides that feature.
Tip: If you want to clear another app’s data, open Settings → Apps (Android) or iPhone Storage (iOS).

Duplicates cleanup

Contacts and photos are scanned on-device. Deleting is always confirmed.

Django backend (optional)

You can configure a backend URL (example: https://api.yoursite.com:8001) to receive privacy event logs.
