In-app deletion steps

  1. Open Privacy Vault PRO on your device.
  2. Open the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete all my data.
  4. Confirm. The app resets local logs, preferences, avatars, cache, API key, and device identifier.

Web / email deletion

Email zub165@yahoo.com with subject Delete My Data. Include whether you used Android or iOS. We complete requests within 30 days.

What is removed vs what stays

  • Removed: in-app logs, preferences (except you may reinstall fresh), stored API key/URL, app cache/documents created by Privacy Vault PRO, optional backend events when requested.
  • Stays on device: contacts/photos you did not ask the app to delete; other apps’ data; system settings outside this app.

Privacy Vault PRO does not require a username/password account. Uninstalling the app also clears its sandbox data.
